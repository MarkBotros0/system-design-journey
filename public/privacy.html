Privacy Policy

Senior Loop (iOS, Android and web) · Last updated 25 November 2026

Senior Loop is an offline trainer for senior engineering interviews. It has no accounts, no servers of its own and no advertising. In short: we do not collect any data about you.

What the app stores

Your progress (lessons read, practice answers, review schedule, streaks and settings) is saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

If you use Export progress, the app creates a file and hands it to your system's share sheet. Where that file goes is up to you.

What we collect

Nothing. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs, and it does not use your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos.

Notifications

The app does not use push notifications. Any reminder it offers is scheduled locally on your device, with no push service or device token involved.

Third parties

No personal data is shared with or sold to third parties. The web version loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives the standard request information (such as your IP address) any website request carries; the mobile apps bundle their fonts and make no such request.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
